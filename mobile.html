Terms & Conditions

Please review the reminder device policy for library wellness and privacy.

1. Reminder Device Purpose

The FlexScape Reminder Device assists university students during study sessions by providing subtle interval alerts, encouraging regular ergonomic posture shifts and active screen breaks.

2. Library Quiet Environment

All reminders are delivered via soft ambient light pulses and localized micro-haptics. Auditory chimes are automatically silenced in library quiet zones.

3. Privacy, Vision Tracking & Sound Recording

Motion tracking and sound recording for hands-free voice control in the FlexScape Room operate on local edge computing models. Microphone audio is used strictly for real-time local voice command recognition. No sound recordings or webcam video feeds are ever recorded, broadcast, or stored on external servers.

4. UniKey Account Linking

Your university key is used solely for desk booking identification, break scheduling, and personal wellness streak stats.
